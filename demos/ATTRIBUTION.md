# Exercise demos

## Illustrations

Bryl Lim created the visual assets in [Workout Guide](https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide/tree/aac599224bb9780305239607ef98540b7e0ce389/packages/workout-guide), with some source poses adapted from [Everkinetic](https://github.com/everkinetic/data). This app resizes and composites the source frames on a dark background and assembles them into looping GIFs. The originals and these adaptations are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The [license text](licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) is included.
