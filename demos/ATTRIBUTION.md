# Exercise demos

## Illustrations

TITAN’s illustration library uses artwork by Bryl Lim from [workout-guide](https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide). Some first frames adapt illustrations from [Everkinetic](https://github.com/everkinetic/data). The library’s adaptations use a dark background, 360 × 360 output, and a 1–2–3–2 frame sequence at 0.8 seconds per frame. These illustrated exercise GIFs remain licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The [license text](licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) is included.

## Video clips

TITAN’s video GIFs come from namnq’s [workout-free-videos](https://github.com/namnq/workout-free-videos) v2.0.0 (GymPlan clips). The source distributes these clips under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The [dedication text](licenses/CC0-1.0.txt) is included.

## Starter placeholders

The two animated equipment drawings in `placeholders/` were created for this app. They mark exercises awaiting a demonstration. Both drawings are offered under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
